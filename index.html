01 · The challenge

Knowing when grain is truly dry
is harder than it looks.

THE DRYING PROBLEM

Large areas. Uneven conditions. One important decision.

Farmers spread, turn, and mix harvested grain over large drying areas. But moisture does not always dry evenly, making it difficult to know when the grain is actually ready.

Touch, appearance, experience, or separate measuring tools can all leave room for uncertainty.

Large drying area→Uneven moisture→Uncertain readiness
The real challenge: knowing when to stop drying.
SPREAD→TURN→DRY→?
Moisture varies across the same batch

What if the tool used for turning the grain could also tell you when it is ready?

03 · The innovation

The measurement happens
while the work happens.

SmartDry integrates sensing into the tool farmers already use to mix, turn, and spread grain. As grain is handled, the system captures conditions and evaluates drying readiness.

Built into a familiar actionThere is no separate checking routine. Farmers use the same motion they already trust for everyday grain drying.
Designed for real field conditionsInstead of asking farmers to judge invisible moisture by appearance alone, SmartDry brings useful guidance to the drying surface.
Practical intelligence, not complexityThe technology stays in the background and gives a simple answer at the moment it matters.
